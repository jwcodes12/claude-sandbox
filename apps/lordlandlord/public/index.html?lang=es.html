Frequently Asked Questions

Is Stealr.io free to play?

Yes! Stealr.io is completely free to play in your browser. No download or account required.

How many players can play?

Stealr.io supports 2 to 4 players per game. You can also play against AI bots.

What kind of game is Stealr.io?

Stealr.io is an online property trading card game where you collect rent, steal properties, and use action cards to be the first to complete 3 full property sets.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just open stealr.io in your browser, enter your name, and start playing instantly.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes! Stealr.io works on any device — desktop, tablet, or mobile. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App.

Is there a mobile app?

Yes! Stealr.io is available on the App Store for iOS and on Google Play for Android. You can also install the web app to your homescreen on any device.

Is Stealr.io the same as Monopoly Deal?

Stealr.io is inspired by property trading card games like Monopoly Deal. It features similar mechanics — collecting rent, stealing properties, and completing property sets — in a free online multiplayer format.

How do rent cards work?

Rent cards let you collect money from opponents based on how many properties you own of a specific color. The more properties in a set, the higher the rent. Double Rent cards can double the amount.

What are the card types?

Stealr.io has 106 cards: Money, Property (10 colors), Action cards (Pass Go, Birthday, Debt Collector), Rent cards, Theft cards (Deal Breaker, Sly Deal, Forced Deal), and Wildcards/Jokers.

Can I play against AI bots?

Yes! Stealr.io offers 3 bot difficulty levels — Easy, Medium, and Hard — so you can practice or play a quick game anytime.

What is the best strategy?

Bank money early, complete small sets first, save Just Say No for critical moments, and use Pass Go immediately. Read our full strategy guide for advanced tips.

How do wildcards and jokers work?

Wildcards count as a property for any color. Rainbow jokers fit all 10 colors, while dual jokers can flip between 2 colors. Once in a complete set, they're protected from theft.
